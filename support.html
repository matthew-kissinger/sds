Support

Report bugs and share beta feedback.

Sheep Dog Sim is in a web-only v2.6 beta. Reports that include browser, device, scene, mode, and what happened before the issue are the fastest to act on.

Contact

Report bugs, accessibility issues, and beta feedback through GitHub Issues. Include public details only; do not post account, privacy, or security-sensitive information in an issue.

For privacy, security, press, or playtest coordination, use the contact route listed on Matthew Kissinger's GitHub profile until a dedicated Sheep Dog Sim mailbox is published.

Useful bug details

  • Browser and version, for example Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
  • Device type, graphics hardware if known, and whether this was mobile or desktop.
  • Scene and mode, such as Home Field Classic or Rolling Hills Chaos.
  • Whether the issue happened in solo play, multiplayer, tutorial, leaderboard, or settings.
  • A screenshot, console error, or short screen recording when it is easy to capture.

Beta notes

The public v2.6 beta focuses on three scenes: Home Field, Rolling Hills, and Open Country. Newsheepdogland is a gated lab for directed testing and is not part of the public beta promise.

Leaderboards are active beta infrastructure. Scores may be reset, archived, or moved into seasonal views before launch if that produces a cleaner competition.
