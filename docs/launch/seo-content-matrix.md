## FAQ

1. Is Sheep Dog Sim free to play?
   - Yes. It is free in the browser, with no signup, ads, or microtransactions.
2. Does Sheep Dog Sim have multiplayer?
   - Yes. It supports 2-4 player online co-op and competitive rooms.
3. Can I play Sheep Dog Sim on mobile?
   - Yes. It has touch controls and responsive HUD support in modern mobile browsers.
4. What is Newsheepdogland?
   - Newsheepdogland is a gated survival lab. It is not part of the three public launch scenes until it is ungated.
5. Is the source available?
   - Yes. Current source is AGPL-3.0-or-later and current assets are CC BY-SA 4.0.

## No-Go Claims

- Do not say the game is on Steam, App Store, Google Play, CrazyGames, Poki, or other portals until Cycle 110 review approves publication.
- Do not market Newsheepdogland, survival wolves, or co-op survival as public launch features while the scene remains gated.
- Do not claim native desktop is publicly released.
- Do not claim mobile WebGPU is universally ready.
- Do not claim online multiplayer works without network access.
- Do not use old peaceful-meadow-only, four-public-scene, survival-headline, three-biome, or 200-sheep-only copy.

## v2.6.0 Beta Decisions

- Public copy uses `Sheep Dog Sim`; `Sheep Dog Simulator` remains an accepted search alternate and possible domain redirect.
- Rolling Hills is the default social visual while Newsheepdogland is gated.
- Keep AGPL/source in details, about, and required notices; do not lead short player copy with licensing.
- Web beta is free/no ads/no microtransactions. Itch donationware and Steam pricing are deferred.
