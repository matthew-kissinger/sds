# Sheep Dog Sim v2.6.0 Beta Release Notes

Status: release source copy for the v2.6.0 web beta.

## Headline

Sheep Dog Sim v2.6.0 beta focuses the public web game around three playable scenes, clearer first-session onboarding, multiplayer rooms, leaderboard discovery, and a cleaner beta feedback loop.

## Player-Facing Notes

- Three public scenes: Home Field, Rolling Hills, and Open Country.
- Newsheepdogland remains a gated lab for directed testing and is not part of the public beta promise.
- Bark remains a core herding skill with tutorial coverage and HUD feedback.
- Multiplayer rooms remain available for 2-4 player co-op and competitive play.
- Leaderboards remain active beta infrastructure; seasonal boards are planned as a follow-up unless implemented before release.
- Support and privacy pages are available at `https://sheepdogsim.com/support` and `https://sheepdogsim.com/privacy`.
- The beta stays web-only on `https://sheepdogsim.com`; itch, Steam, and portal submissions are deferred.

## Release Checklist Link

Use [`release-checklist.md`](release-checklist.md) for final validation, tagging, production deploy proof, and rollback steps.

## Do Not Claim

- Four public scenes.
- Public survival/wolves mode.
- Steam, itch, portal, native, or paid-store availability.
- Steam leaderboards, Steam networking, Steam Cloud, achievements, or ad monetization.
