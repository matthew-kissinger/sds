## Medium (Reddit r/WebGames, r/incremental_games style)

I ship a browser herding sim where you are the dog. v2.3.0 just landed:
a sixty-second first-run tutorial (skippable, never asks twice), nine
achievements, full key and gamepad rebinding, colorblind mode, and the
tutorial now speaks English, Spanish, Japanese, Portuguese, and Chinese.
Under the hood the multiplayer server only sends the sheep that moved,
which is most of them, until you start penning. One fenced pasture and
three islands, six modes, five dogs, up to 5,000 sheep. Free, in the
browser: sheepdogsim.com
