# Sheep Dog Sim v2.6.2 Controller Camera Hotfix Release Notes

Status: release source copy for the v2.6.2 web beta hotfix.

Sheep Dog Sim v2.6.2 keeps the v2.6.0 web beta posture and improves controller, laptop keyboard, and first-run tutorial camera flow.

## Changed

- Controllers can now operate the entrance menu and first-run tutorial offer. Start/A confirm the current menu choice.
- Camera zoom is available during active solo play from laptop ArrowUp/ArrowDown keys and standard gamepad A/B buttons.
- The first-run tutorial now opens on Home Field in a close Follow camera behind the dog before introducing camera switching and zoom.

## Fixed

- Controller confirm no longer falls through from the visible tutorial offer to the underlying Play button.
- Bark hint and bark meter UI stand down while tutorial prompts own the bottom of the screen.

## Validation

- Local unit, build, typecheck, and focused controller/camera/UI gates passed.
- Production-preview Playwright probes verified controller menu accept, Start-to-play, keyboard zoom, gamepad zoom, and close tutorial Follow framing.
