Title: v2.3.0 - the game teaches you to herd now

---

Sheep Dog Sim v2.3.0 is live at sheepdogsim.com. Most of this release is
the game finally surfacing things it should have had from the start: a
tutorial, achievements, finished settings, and a first load that does not
make you wait.

THE TUTORIAL

First time in, the game offers a guided sixty seconds on Home Field:
move, sprint, swap the camera, pen three sheep. Skippable, and it never
asks twice. The sheep do not always cooperate. That is the lesson.

ACHIEVEMENTS

Nine to start: pen the flock on Home Field, Rolling Hills, and Open
Country, finish Solo Chaos (5,000 sheep), survive one night and then five
nights on Newsheepdogland, win a competitive multiplayer room, and finish
a round with all five dogs. Your dogs wear their badges on the menu
screen.

SETTINGS, FINISHED

Rebind every key. Plug in a gamepad and rebind that too. Colorblind
mode. A language picker with English, Spanish, Japanese, Portuguese, and
Chinese; the tutorial speaks all five.

FASTER FIRST LOAD

The entrance is visible in 0.9 seconds on a 20 Mbps connection, down
from 8.8. The world still streams in behind it, but you are looking at
the island instead of a spinner.

MULTIPLAYER PLUMBING

The server now sends only the sheep that changed since the last frame.
As a round progresses and sheep are penned, bandwidth drops to less than
half of what it was. Behind that: a 100-room load test with zero
desyncs across 208,000 server ticks, and connections that stop keeping
up get evicted instead of stalling the room for everyone else.

INVITE LINKS

Send a URL, your friend lands in your lobby. There is also a share
button if you want to show off a score.

AND THE QUIET STUFF

If the game crashes it now reports the stack trace so I can fix it. If
your GPU resets mid-game the renderer recovers instead of going black.
If a new version ships while you have a tab open, a toast offers the
update instead of leaving you on stale code.
