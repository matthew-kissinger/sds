# Sheep Dog Sim v2.6.4 Survival UI Ownership Release Notes

Status: release source copy for the v2.6.4 web beta hotfix.

Sheep Dog Sim v2.6.4 removes Survival-only time controls from Home Field and every other non-survival scene.

## Fixed

- The Midday/day-phase chip appears only on the Survival scene.
- Skip to Dusk appears only on the Survival scene.
- Home Field no longer initializes Survival phases, wolves, economy, gate scheduling, or HUD controls.
- Home Field's long ambient sun cycle and farmhouse dusk lamp remain intact.

## Validation

- A scene-registry regression test proves Newsheepdogland is the sole owner of the Survival day loop.
- A Chromium gameplay test starts Home Field Just Play and asserts that the game is active while the Survival run, day loop, day/phase chip, and Skip to Dusk control are all absent.
- The rendered Home Field gameplay screenshot was inspected with the normal HUD intact and no Survival time controls visible.
- The full 2,403-test suite, production build, lint, and typecheck pass.
