# Original synthesis audio review

Updated 2026-08-24.

## Release boundary

The former provider-generated MP3 set is not part of Sheepdog Sim 3. Every
shipped media sound is now generated by `tools/bake-audio.mjs`, a dependency-free
Node recipe committed with the game. The bake uses fixed seeds and writes mono
22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV. It uses no recording, sample library, downloaded
asset, model, account, network request, external encoder or third-party media.

The source and output are AGPL-3.0-or-later. `assets/audio/manifest.json` records
the recipe version, synthesis description, seed, duration, format, size,
SHA-256, peak, RMS level and loop-seam delta for every file.

## Mix iteration record

The first 600-second synthesis capture was mechanically valid and measured
-28.1 LUFS integrated with 2.3 LU loudness range, compared with -39.2 LUFS and
15.3 LU in the prior no-wind candidate. Event counts were essentially unchanged
at 1,069 versus 1,075. That difference initially suggested the new foreground
sheep-call masters were too hot.

A controlled diagnostic bake lowered only those three calls by about 9 dB.
Its 600-second result was -28.5 LUFS with the same 2.3 LU range and 1,070 voices.
That disproves the sheep-master hypothesis. EBU integrated gating and the
continuous layer bed dominate this comparison, so the long LUFS number must not
be presented as a foreground-call result. The diagnostic change was reverted;
the release source is byte-identical to the committed first bake. Both receipts
remain under `captures/audio/v3-original-synth-fatigue-iteration-1/` and
`captures/audio/v3-original-synth-fatigue-iteration-2/` for audit.

## Critic questions

Judge the running mix, not isolated WAV files.

1. With eyes closed, does the field read as open, calm and inhabited?
2. Do the three sheep voices read as stylized sheep rather than an oscillator?
3. Do the three bark voices read as one collie with useful variation?
4. Does agitation become audible through crowd density and foreground calls?
5. Are footfalls present without becoming a fast metronome while sprinting?
6. Do birds, leaves, crowd, pant and chime avoid a detectable short loop?
7. Does the quiet mix remain intelligible on laptop speakers and headphones?
8. Does ten minutes expose buzz, a rough high-frequency opening, a repeated
   phrase or listening fatigue?
9. Does the pentatonic progress phrase remain warm as penned count rises, and
   does the completion resolve land as a sigh rather than a reward sting?

## Acceptance status

Provenance, deterministic rebake, runtime integration, lifecycle, budget and
metering pass. Animal-call character and the ten-minute listening experience
are not self-accepted by automated checks. They remain a separate audio-critic
and owner-listening gate. If the synthesized sheep or bark voices fail that
gate, the safest reduced launch is the proven ambient, footfall, huff, bell,
gate, fence and Web Audio tone set with foreground animal calls muted. Do not
restore the removed provider media.
