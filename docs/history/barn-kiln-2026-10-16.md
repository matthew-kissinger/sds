# The Kiln barn, 2026-10-16

Release 2, the Kiln barn item. A second Kiln building, after the farmhouse: a
recipe under `assets/barn/`, baked with the per-vertex surface channels pull
request 121 established, welded into the cluster's own buffers, and shipping
behind a `BARN_SOURCE` constant (`app/src/scene/farmhouse/barnSource.ts`) that
stays `'procedural'`. The flip is a separate commit, after the owner's look
review and a hub receipt, with a grass rebake. Nothing in the house, its
loader, its constant or its spec was touched.

## The design, against spec/05

**Silhouette first.** In flat black (`06-silhouette`) the outline is a gambrel
with a cupola and a vane, which reads as a barn before anything else does. The
procedural barn's is a plain gable with a lean-to and a cupola (`06-silhouette`
under `barn/`).

**The ramp needs a second value in the roof, and a gambrel is how this sun gets
one.** The sun is 8 degrees up in the north-west and both cameras look north,
so the door elevation and every south slope of ordinary pitch sit in the
shadow band. The procedural barn's 38.5 degree gable is half-lambert 0.35 on
its south slope: one dark slab over one dark wall from the approach. The Kiln
roof's three planes a side, south, at the barn's yaw under `SUN_DIRECTION`:

| Plane | Pitch | Half-lambert | Band |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| flare, z 6.0 to 5.4 | 30.3 | 0.39 | shadow |
| skirt, z 5.4 to 2.9 | 60.0 | 0.25 | shadow |
| cap, z 2.9 to 0 | 22.0 | 0.44 | body |

The shadow band ends at 0.42 behind a 0.07 terminator. A first cut at 25.8
degrees put the cap on the terminator's midpoint, half a band; 22 is the
shallowest that still sheds as slate and puts nine-tenths of the body band
into it. `01-classic` shows the result: a lighter cap over a dark skirt, the
two meeting at the knee.

**Palette.** Every material is named after a band set and the game re-binds by
name, so no colour in the recipe reaches the screen: they exist so a Kiln
contact sheet looks approximately right. Rust-red is the `barn` set, the slate
the existing `BARN_ROOF`, the trim the cluster's timber, the sill wall its
dressed stone.

**Outlines on the hero objects.** The barn is outlined like the house, by the
cluster's inverted hull, and the hull is grown from masses the bake records
(below) rather than from a hand copy.

**Chunky, confident shapes.** Flat-normalled boards and boxes, no bevels, trims
that stand 4 cm proud so they catch a lit edge, a stone sill wall 8 cm proud of
the boards so its top reads as a light line at the foot of a shaded wall.

**No baked light, no mixed provenance.** No texture, no image, no external
mesh. The two channels are geometry (metres below a roof edge, metres along a
slope), not light.

**The inside is painted as the opening.** The game has no shadow map, so a
floor seen through a door is lit as if it stood in the field. Every surface
inside the shell takes the `opening` set, the cluster's warm dark: through the
doors the barn reads as a dark volume with forms in it (floor, bents, lofts,
the lantern's glow), not a lit room. The hay on the west mow, where the low sun
does reach through the hay door, is timber; the lantern and the loft window are
lamps, which spec/05's post gives a glow.

**Why it stands in the procedural barn's box.** 16.5 by 10.8 m to the boards and
a 10.5 m ridge are `BARN` in `plan.ts`. Keeping them means the pad, the yaw,
the cast shadow (`BARN`'s box, which this barn fills to the centimetre) and
the farmer's route are the same for either source, so the flip changes the
building and nothing around it.

## The channel convention, and where it comes from

TEXCOORD_0 carries the two channels and the root node's extras carry
`surfaceChannels` (version 1, attribute TEXCOORD_0): the convention
`assets/house/farmhouse.kiln.js` established at 523bd7a5, in main since the
merge of pull request 121 (`00fdc295`). This branch read it against the house
recipe at 813424d0 and main at e07303af. The shader graph is unchanged
(`farmhouse/surfaceMaterial.ts`); the barn uses it as follows:

- `uv.x`: 1 on slate, which selects `BARN_ROOF` in the roof set; 0 elsewhere.
- `uv.y` on boards and timber: metres below the roof underside directly over
  the face - a soffit, the ceiling, the hood's or the cupola cap's - by a
  vertical ray from a fifth of a millimetre off the face, held on its own
  triangle's side of every plane where an underside bends or ends. Faces are
  split first at the ridge, the knee and flare mitres, the hood's shoulders,
  the eave edge and the verges, so the distance is linear across each
  triangle. Negative only where a face runs up out of sight (a gable top 0.06 m
  into the slab, the cupola's head 0.08 m into its cap's skirt), 9 indoors and
  where no roof is over the face.
- `uv.y` on slate: metres along the slate from its eave edge, straight across
  the knee - 0.695 m of flare, 5.693 m at the knee, 8.821 m at the ridge - so
  the cap's courses and sky ramp carry on from the skirt's instead of
  restarting at a second eave. -1 on the ridge roll, the slab's cut edges and
  the cupola cap.
- 9 on stone, 0 on openings and lamps.
- Every distance is rounded to the millimetre. That is finer than anything the
  shader resolves, and it is what lets the export weld the corners triangles
  share: before the rounding, samples that differed in their last digits at a
  shared corner left the bake at 203,456 bytes.

`tests/barn-kiln.spec.ts` asserts every gable vertex against the slab
underside the bake records, to 5 mm, knee included; the slate's 5.693 at the
knee and 8.821 at the ridge; the selectors; and the loader's refusals.

## The outline envelope, written by the bake

The follow-up the farmhouse note left open - have the recipe describe its roof
rather than keep a copy in `plan.ts` - is done for the barn. The recipe writes
`outlineEnvelope` (version 1) beside `surfaceChannels`:

- `slab`: the slate's outer surface and its underside as one section across
  the ridge, eave edge to eave edge, extruded between the verges (x +/-8.7);
- `boxes`: the walls from the foot to the underside at the wall line, and the
  cupola's body;
- `caps`: the cupola's cap, a square pyramid.

`farmhouse/hull.ts` `envelopeHull` grows the outline from it: the slab's upper
half only (its top face and a face halfway to the underside), closed at both
verges, the boxes without a floor, the cap with its eave square drawn in 5 cm.
Half of the 200 mm slab plus the outline's 90 mm push stays inside the slate,
so no ledge hangs under the eaves - the fault the house's hand-copied envelope
had. `kilnBarn.ts` refuses a bake without the record, or with a malformed one,
by name. The tests hold the record to the bake (every section corner is a baked
slate or soffit vertex within 2 mm, the cupola's body and cap bounds within a
millimetre) and the hull inside the slab, and check that the slab closes.

## The ground

**Footprint**, in the barn's own frame, written into the GLB as `footprint` and
repeated in `plan.ts` as `KILN_BARN_FOOTPRINT` for the grass bake, which does
not read a GLB; the spec holds the copy to the record:

| Rectangle | x | z |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `barn`: the walls and the stone sill wall, 0.08 proud | -8.33 to 8.33 | -5.48 to 5.48 |
| `barn apron`: stone under the swing of both leaves | -3.10 to 4.00 | -7.50 to -5.48 |
| `barn step`: at the stable door | 4.60 to 6.00 | -5.90 to -5.48 |

Nothing of the barn touches the ground outside these (asserted on every vertex
below 0.3 m). The apron's south-east corner is the barn's nearest point to the
pasture and lands on the pad's south edge at z 135.

**Levels.** Walls, plinth, apron and step start at -0.05, a hand's depth under
the pad. The threshing floor is at 0.15, the step the farmhouse took for the
same reason: a floor in the pad's plane fights the terrain through the doors.
The apron and step tops are at 0.14 and the threshold board at 0.20.

**Z-fighting: none seen.** Looking in through the cart doors from 12 m
(`03-cart-doors`) and at the Classic bearing from 90 m (`01-classic`, enlarged
4x over the apron and the door), on WebGL2 and WebGPU, the floor, the apron, the
threshold and the plinth draw clean. The apron's top is 2 cm over the cast
shadow's alpha slab (0.12, `farmhouse/shadows.ts`) and occludes it without
striping at either distance.

**Grass.** `tools/bake-grass.mjs` now reads `BARN_SOURCE` beside
`FARMHOUSE_SOURCE` and cuts the barn that ships: the procedural barn and its
lean-to, or the three rectangles above. The manifest records `barnSource` next
to `farmhouseSource`, and `tests/grass-bake.spec.ts` fails, naming the rebake,
when it disagrees with the constant, as it already did for the house. **Rebaked
for what main ships - the Kiln house since #124, the procedural barn - the bake
is byte-identical to main's**: `tufts.bin` compares equal, and the manifest
differs by the one added `barnSource` line. Before the house flip the same held
with both constants procedural. A bake for the Kiln barn cuts barn 8, apron 0,
step 0 tufts with either house; the procedural barn and lean-to cut 7 and 2
under the Kiln house (98,291 tufts in all; 98,292 with the Kiln barn), 8 and 1
under the procedural one.

**The farmer's route**, as walked, against the baked geometry: 2.41 m clear of
the walls' footprint, 1.25 m of the nearest point of an open leaf at body
height, 1.02 m of the apron's corner, 2.32 m of the step. The spec asserts more

## The slate courses wave, on both pitches

The coordinator flagged wavy courses on the gambrel's upper pitch. They are
still there, and they are not the channel: they are the shared slate graph's
`wander` (`farmhouse/surfaceMaterial.ts`, `ROOF_COURSE_WANDER` 0.16), which
shifts every course line by up to 0.16 of a course - about 0.15 m along the
slope - by a painted field over world position whose main wavelength along the
ridge is about 11 m. Every slate in the cluster carries it: the house's, the
procedural barn's, this barn's skirt and its cap. Measured, not argued: with
the wander set to 0 for one capture (and put back), the courses on both
pitches run straight (`captures/barn/diag-wander0/compare-04-shipped-vs-wander0.png`,
shipped left, zero right).

What the slate channel running straight across the knee does is different: the
cap's courses carry on counting from the skirt's and its sky ramp stays at the
ridge tone. Restarted at the knee, the cap would take a second eave - a dark
band 0.9 m up from the knee and its own course phase - which is the fault the
continuous channel prevents.

Straightening them is a shader decision, and the house passed its look review
with this shader: lower the wander for every slate, or scale it by the slate
selector so the barn slate (`uv.x` 1, which the procedural barn's slate also
carries) runs straighter, or stretch the field's wavelength along the ridge.
None was made here.
