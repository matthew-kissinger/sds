Privacy

No signup, no ads, no payments.

Sheep Dog Sim is a browser game. The beta uses local browser storage, Cloudflare-hosted services, telemetry events, multiplayer room data, and public leaderboard entries.

Last updated

June 30, 2026.

What is collected

  • Game settings and local progress may be saved in your browser storage.
  • Telemetry events may record game state such as scene, mode, renderer, errors, and completion results.
  • Multiplayer uses room, invite, and connection data needed to run live sessions.
  • Leaderboards store submitted names, scene, mode, score, time, and related run metadata.
  • Cloudflare may process technical request data such as IP address, user agent, and performance/security logs.
  • Email support collects whatever contact details and report content you send.

What is not collected

  • No account is required to play.
  • No payment information is collected by Sheep Dog Sim.
  • No advertising profile is sold by Sheep Dog Sim.
  • No microphone, camera, or precise location permission is required for normal play.

Beta leaderboards

Leaderboard names and scores are public when submitted. During beta, leaderboard data may be reset, archived, or split into seasonal and all-time views as the competition format is tuned.

Telemetry choice

Product telemetry can be turned off from the in-game Settings menu. Score submission, multiplayer room operation, leaderboard identity, security, and anti-abuse logs may still be required when you choose those features.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the contact route listed on Matthew Kissinger's GitHub profile. Include the leaderboard name, scene, mode, and approximate submission time if the request is about a score.
