Sheep Dog Sim

Guide your sheepdog across one fenced pasture and three hand-built islands and try to herd up to 5,000 sheep. Free, browser-based, no install.

Four biomes, survival wolves, six modes, real-time multiplayer for 2-4 players. Open the tab, herd some sheep, close the tab. No ads, no signup, source-readable under AGPL-3.0.

Biomes

  • Home Field. Flat fenced pasture with a perimeter pen. The starter.
  • Rolling Hills. 180-metre island, golden-hour mood, lightning-zap corral.
  • Open Country. 380-metre island; gather sheep, then drive them through a magical portal.
  • Newsheepdogland. Boot-shaped survival island with wolves after dark.

Modes

  • Just Play. 30 sheep, no timer, no fail state.
  • Solo Classic. 200 sheep with leaderboard.
  • Solo Extreme. 1,000 sheep.
  • Solo Insane. 3,000 sheep.
  • Solo Chaos. 5,000 sheep.
  • Survival. Start with 10 sheep, bring them home before the wolves thin the flock.
  • Multiplayer. 2-4 player co-op, competitive, and timed rooms with shareable invite URLs.
