Sheep Dog Sim

Guide your sheepdog across three public scenes and try to herd up to 5,000 sheep. Free browser game, no install, no signup, no ads.

Solo challenges, real-time multiplayer for 2-4 players, mobile controls, gamepad support, and source-readable AGPL-3.0 code.

Public scenes and lab

  • Home Field. Flat fenced pasture with a perimeter pen. The starter.
  • Rolling Hills. 180-metre island, golden-hour mood, fenced pasture with one gate.
  • Open Country. 380-metre island; gather sheep, then drive them through a magical portal.
  • Newsheepdogland. Gated lab for directed testing of larger experimental systems.

Modes

  • Just Play. Learn sheep pressure with no timer.
  • Solo Classic. 200 sheep with leaderboard.
  • Solo Extreme. 1,000 sheep.
  • Solo Insane. 3,000 sheep.
  • Solo Chaos. 5,000 sheep.
  • Multiplayer. 2-4 player co-op, competitive, and timed rooms with shareable invite URLs.
