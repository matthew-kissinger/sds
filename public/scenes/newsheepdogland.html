Gated beta lab

Newsheepdogland

A boot-shaped large-scene lab under a low dusk sky. A mountain in the north, a wide grazing foot, a homestead pen, and experimental pressure systems for directed testing only.

Why it is gated

The v2.6 beta keeps the public game focused on Home Field, Rolling Hills, and Open Country. Newsheepdogland exists to test larger scene scale, homestead routing, day/night pressure, and experimental flock behavior without making those systems a public launch promise.

Directed testers may receive specific lab links or instructions. Public players should use the three public scenes while this work is tuned, profiled, and validated.

Scene shape

  • Boot-shaped island, roughly 3.2 square kilometres.
  • A 120-metre mountain in the northern leg, a wide grazing foot to the south.
  • A homestead with a real pen fence and a single day-cycled gate.
  • Forest, tall-grass shore bands, and a coastline the sim and the render share.

The large-scene read

Newsheepdogland is the large-scene read for the desktop WebGPU path: the Hosek-Wilkie sky, shoreline water, homestead props, and GPU compute-culled grass and trees. Mobile still keeps conservative fallback paths where device budgets require them.
