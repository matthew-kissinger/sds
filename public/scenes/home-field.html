Starter biome

Home Field

A flat fenced pasture with one gate. No terrain tricks, no hidden shoreline, just the cleanest place to learn how the flock reacts.

Why this scene exists

Home Field is the baseline. Before the islands add slopes, trees, water, and long drives, this scene lets you read the core behavior: how sheep cluster, where they break, and how much pressure your dog can apply before the flock spills sideways.

Just Play starts here with 30 sheep and no fail state. Classic adds a 200-sheep leaderboard run once you want timing pressure.

Scene shape

  • Flat fenced pasture with a single gate.
  • Short sightlines and no slope-modulated movement.
  • Best first map for learning dog pressure and flock recovery.

How to play

Move with WASD or arrow keys. Hold Shift to sprint. On mobile, use the bottom-left joystick and sprint button. Get behind the flock and drive it through the gate.
