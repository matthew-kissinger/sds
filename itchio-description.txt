Sheep Dog Sim is a free 3D herding game where you guide a sheepdog around a living flock, cut off escapes, and drive sheep home.

Play a quiet starter pasture, golden-hour island herding, Open Country portal runs, or Newsheepdogland survival after dark when wolves start thinning the flock. Modes range from 30-sheep practice to 5,000-sheep chaos, with 2-4 player online rooms, mobile controls, and gamepad support.

Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to move, Shift to sprint, mouse wheel or plus/minus to zoom.
Mobile: touch joystick, sprint button, responsive HUD.

The sheep are not on rails. They flock, separate, recover, and route around terrain obstacles. Your dog applies pressure, so the trick is circling wide, nudging gently, and reading the herd before it breaks.

Free to play. No signup. No ads. No microtransactions.
