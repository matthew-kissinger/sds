Guide your sheepdog through rolling meadows and herd 200 fluffy, panicky sheep through the gate. Sounds simple? The sheep have other ideas.

Sheep flee when you get close. Get too close and the whole flock scatters. The trick is circling wide, nudging them gently, reading the herd. It's surprisingly meditative once you get the rhythm.


WHAT'S IN THE BOX

Five dogs with different playstyles. Jep is balanced, Sally is fast but tires quickly, Pip has long range but moves slower. Pick one that fits how you play.

Classic mode has no timer. Just vibes. Timed mode is for when you want to actually sweat. Multiplayer lets you herd with (or against) friends.

There's a leaderboard if you're competitive about sheep.


HOW IT WORKS

Desktop: WASD moves, Shift sprints, scroll zooms
Mobile: Joystick on the left, sprint button above it
Works in any modern browser


THE SHEEP AI

200 sheep running boid flocking algorithms - they stick together, avoid each other, and align their movement. When your dog approaches, they scatter realistically. It's oddly satisfying to watch.


TIPS

Sheep flee from the dog - use this to guide them
Don't get too close or the flock will scatter
Circle around the flock to keep them together
Sprint drains stamina - use it wisely
