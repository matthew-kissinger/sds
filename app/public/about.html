About the game

One dog. One flock. One field.

Sheepdog Sim is a focused browser game about reading a flock, applying pressure carefully and guiding every sheep through the gate.

Why this rebuild exists

The earlier game proved the simulation and the feel of herding, but years of modes and overlapping systems made the client difficult to change safely. This rebuild keeps the proven simulation and focuses the experience on one field and one clear path through the code.

The initial release is single-player with 25, 75 or 200 sheep. It works on desktop and mobile and uses WebGPU with a WebGL2 fallback.

Open source

The source code is available under AGPL-3.0-or-later. The repository includes the simulation, renderer, input, audio system, deterministic asset recipes and release tests.
